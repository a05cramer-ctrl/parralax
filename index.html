    How it works

    Who
    The top 50 wallets by 30-day PnL on the public Hyperliquid leaderboard, with at least $100k account value. Vaults are excluded.
    What
    Every open perp position: side, size, entry, uPnL, leverage and liquidation price.
    Refresh
    Positions every 10 s while this tab is open. Marks stream live over the Hyperliquid websocket.
    Convergence
    Each wallet votes with its 5 biggest positions. A convergence is 5+ wallets on the same side of one coin, at least double the other side. Alerts fire only when a new cluster forms between two refreshes.
    Open time
    Read from each wallet's fills in the last 24 h. Older entries show as opened more than 24 h ago.
